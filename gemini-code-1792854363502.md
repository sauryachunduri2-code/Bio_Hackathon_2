# OmniBank Blindspot Explorer 🧬
**IMET Hackathon 2026 — Genomic Equity Track**  
**Partners:** Major League Hacking (MLH) & Google Gemini  

## Overview
The **OmniBank Blindspot Explorer** is an interactive, high-fidelity web dashboard built to expose global imbalances in genomic data repositories and explain their direct clinical hazards, policy implications, and patient-level consequences.

## Key Features
1. **Hero & 30-Second Hook:** Immediate visualization of the 75% vs 16% European descent over-indexing imbalance.
2. **Interactive Paired Chart:** Visualizing Global vs. Biobank percentages across 6 major ancestry demographics.
3. **Representation Heatmap Matrix:** Clickable regional cards mapping representation ratios and severity status.
4. **Deep Ethics & Clinical Panel:** Research-supported analysis of diagnostic failure rates, VUS bottlenecks, and polygenic risk score inaccuracies.
5. **+5 Bonus Stretch Challenge:** Integrated historical case studies and precedents (e.g., Manrai et al., NEJM 2016; Martin et al., Nature Genetics 2019).
6. **Gemini Explainer Integration:** AI-powered plain-language clinical breakdown with robust fallback support.
7. **Live Clinic Simulator:** Interactive parity slider calculating sample recruitment deficits in real-time.

## Tech Stack
- Pure HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Chart.js (Zero build dependencies for robust deployment).
- Deployed on Vercel.
